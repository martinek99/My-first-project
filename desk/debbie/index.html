berries, a little potato, a measured scoop of beans or quinoa
A thumb of fat
olive oil, avocado, nuts, or cheese — measured, not poured

Why this helps after a hysterectomy

The body often holds weight after that surgery. Muscle wants more protein. Bones want calcium. Crash diets make both worse. This week is protein-first, vegetable-heavy, and calm on the carbs so she can lose fat without starving.

Why this helps diabetes

  • Same three meals every day, plus one optional snack. No all-day picking.
  • No juice, no regular soda, no sweet tea, no flavored yogurt.
  • Bread, pasta, rice, and potatoes show up only when the recipe says so, and in a measured amount.
  • Water, black coffee, or unsweetened tea with meals.

If she does not like a meal

Her doctor still comes first.

This is a kitchen plan, not a prescription. If her doctor or dietitian set a different carb number, medicine time, or kidney limit, follow them. The calories and grams here are estimates. If she feels dizzy, shaky, or her sugar runs low, she should eat a planned carb and call her doctor — do not just skip meals.
