The Hunt

Free or cheap within about 100 miles of Redding. Keep it or flip it. I cannot scrape Facebook or Craigslist. I can watch the public boards and hand you the 20 that look worth the gas.

Monday Aug 17 · first pass · confirm before you drive

The honest limit

Facebook Marketplace needs your login. Their rules say no robots. Craigslist’s public feed blocked this chair. I will not write a sneak-around bot, and I will not take your Facebook password.

What I can do: tap-open searches you already use, plus a daily short list from neighbor boards I can actually see. You message them in the app. Free stuff dies in hours. If it is still up, go.

How I score

I throw out mattresses (bugs), pets, “free dental,” and anything that smells like a shipping scam. I keep tools, chairs people pay for, working appliances, hitch gear, stoves if you can haul, and oddball outdoor stuff. Mount Shasta, Dunsmuir, Weed, Chico, Oroville, and Gridley stay in the 100-mile circle. Humboldt does not.

Today’s 20

Public neighbor boards as of Monday morning. Titles move. No phone numbers on this page.

Humanscale Freedom chair — free · Mount Shasta

Those chairs sell used for a few hundred if the cylinder still holds. Sans arms. See it sit before you drive the hour.
