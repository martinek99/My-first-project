JaxParks dog parks

Open sunrise to sunset. Dogs may be off leash only inside a designated dog park. Everywhere else, leash.

Memorial plots

The little plots for ashes and memorials. Take the request here. Confirm current fees and available spaces with the official book before you promise a spot.

Open requests

Pickleball

Most courts are first come, first served, sunrise to sunset. Limit to one hour when others are waiting. Tennis-complex courts can be reserved.

Staffed tennis complexes

Reservations: one court, up to 48 hours ahead, 1½ hours of play. A voicemail is not a reservation. Someone has to call back. Hold 15 minutes. Two players must be there.
