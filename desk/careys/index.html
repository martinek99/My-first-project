How Brittany uses this

1. She looks at the piece.

2. She taps Maker, then the designer. Then Style. Then Color. Then Size. Then Condition. Then the ask.

3. Every list can search. Type a new name and tap Add it. Tap Remove names to take one off.

3b. Ask is three rows: hundreds, tens, ones. Tap 500, then 40, then 7 for $547. Any price from $1 to $999.

4. She taps Copy the whole listing, opens the selling app, and pastes.

5. She still finds the piece, steams it, photographs it, packages it, and ships it. This page only writes the words.

Titles follow her closet: Brand Style in Color Size, with NEW when it is NWT.

Maker names start from her public Poshmark closet, @thecareycurated. I kept every brand she has listed at least three times. One-off brands stay off the list until she types them in.

Can a bot post it for her?

Poshmark, Depop, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace: No honest bot. Those sites do not give regular sellers a “please post this for me” door. Their rules say no robots. A sneak-around bot would need her password. I will not do that.

Poshmark’s own Smart List: Allowed. She takes a photo in their app. Poshmark writes a draft. She still taps Post. That is their official helper, not a third-party bot.

eBay: Yes, honestly. eBay has an official seller door. She would open an eBay seller account, tap Allow once, and then this page could post the listing after she taps through the piece. She still does the physical work. eBay takes a cut. Sizes have to be their standard ones, like S, M, L.

Her own shop: Yes. A simple shop we put on a page she owns. Then posting is just us putting her piece on her page. Buyers have to find her, though. No Poshmark crowd.

Etsy: They have an official door, but it is for vintage (about 20 years old) or handmade. Everyday closet brands like lululemon or Free People usually do not belong there.

Send-it-in places like ThredUp: She boxes the clothes. They list them. Different job. Not her closet voice.

Right now the honest move on Poshmark is: tap, copy, paste. If you want the real one-tap post, say do eBay and I will build that next. She would still have to allow it once in eBay.
